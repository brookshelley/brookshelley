## Books 

### Vineland

Messy in the usual Pynchon way, but the jokes were easier to parse than Gravity’s Rainbow. 

### The Honorable Schoolboy

It’s a pleasure to see how much this influenced so much spy media later. I haven’t read or seen too much media in British-run Hong Kong in the 70s, so that was a neat thing to dive into as well. Interesting that some of the same proxy war stuff is still being fought between the UK, US, Russia, and China.

### The Beyond

Absolutely delightful from the accents of the house workers, to the perverse Italian impression of what New Orleans is, to the over-the-top gorefest. When a movie is completely in English, yet _feels_ dubbed, you know you’re in for a good time. A+ spider foley.
