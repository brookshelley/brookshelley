Ended the month in Japan on a great trip with [Escape Collective](https://www.escapecollective.com). My legs are a bit tired after 10 days of riding, but I'm quite happy with how it went. 750ish km ridden and over 11k meters climbed on the bike between Tokyo and Karuizawa, with a stop in Takayama for the summit. I'll be glad to be home with Snorri again, but despite the blazing heat, this was an all-time great trip. My collarbone barely hurt on the trip, which is a relief.
