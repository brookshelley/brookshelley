## Books

### The Player of Games

A closer look at what the Culture does and doesn't value, even if the individuals in it aren't always aware. 
### Children of Time

Spiders!

### Lords of Uncreation

A fun finish, even if some of the mysteries weren't incredibly satisfying. I'm sold on reading more of Tchaikovsky's SF.

### Eyes of the Void

Returned to the trilogy after two years away. I'm impressed by the characterization and interesting galactic world-building. In a way it reminds me of the culture series.

### Scattered All Over the Earth ￼

A strange story to start a trilogy. I'm not sure how a country could so self-isolate as to disappear completely metaphorically and physically, but as a meditation on the anxiety of the Japanese regarding assimilation and westernization, it works.

### The Wax Child

Olga delivering a truly strange imagining of the events surrounding the murder of many women in 17th century Denmark for "witchcraft".

### Walking Practice

What if gender but alien?!

### Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy

Continuing my goal of reading all Le Carre's with a classic banger.

### There Is No Antimemetics Division

I wasn't familiar with the longstanding SCP stuff, so this was just a fun weird book.

### The Last Tourist

A reread and a still-satisfying conclusion.

### Excession

A great dive into the ship mind politics and weird relationships 

### An American Spy

Still love this series

### Blue Mars

A satisfying conclusion even though the series was a bit long, generally.
